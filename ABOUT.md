This app is a full-stack swim club operations platform for DARE, meant to replace TeamUnify/SportsEngine Motion.

At a high level, it combines:

- Member and household management (parents, swimmers, coaches, groups, registrations)
- Meet operations (meet setup, commitments, entry approvals)
- Meet-file interoperability (`.cl2`, `.sd3`, `.zip` import; Standard/Extended SD3 export; SDIF workflows)
- Performance tracking (results, best times, time standards, progression)
- Billing and payments (invoices, payments, refunds, ledger, Stripe integration)
- Communications (announcements and email campaigns)
- Training and practices (structured workouts, attendance, analytics), volunteer jobs and service hours, club events with calendar feeds, and wellness check-ins
- Role-specific portals: admin/coach dashboard, parent portal, swimmer (athlete) portal, and a public team website; installable as a PWA
- Admin reporting, audit logs, and compliance workflows

So it’s essentially the team’s operational system of record: memberships, meets, money, messaging, and data exchange with swim meet software.
