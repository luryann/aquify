Your club’s front door.

A public page for new families, and an app on every phone. Both come with aquify.

Your name and your storyTell new families who you are.
Join and sign inStraight into aquify.
News and meetsOnly what you choose to show.

Turn it on. Pick what shows. New families find you, and your members sign in from the same page.

You choose what’s public.

1Pick your addressChoose a short web address for your club, like harbor-aquatic.
2Write your about and contactA few lines about the club, plus an email and phone number.
3Tick what showsMark announcements and events as public one by one. Everything else stays inside.
4Turn it off any timeSwitch the page off and it disappears.

What visitors see.

Your club’s name, your story, how to reach you, any announcements and events you’ve marked public, and the next meets. Buttons take families straight to join or sign in.

  • No swimmer names or times ever appear on it.
  • Coach names show only if you turn that on.

One app. Three kinds of phone screen.

Families, coaches and your public page all run from the browser, and install to the home screen.

  • Install itOpen aquify on a phone and add it to the home screen. It opens like an app, with no app store.
  • Works on poor wifiRoll call marks are saved on the phone and sent when the signal returns.
  • One address per clubYour page lives at a short link you can put on a banner or a flyer.
  • Signed-in pages stay privateOnly the public page is visible without a login.
