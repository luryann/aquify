Families are households, not rows.

Each household has its parents, contacts and swimmers in one place. Add a second parent or an emergency contact, and everyone sees the same details.

  • A parent account is the one required login. Swimmers can have their own, but never need one.
  • Swimmers carry their birthday, group and USA Swimming ID, so meet entries fill in correctly.
  • Groups by age and levelSet age ranges and a coach for each group, and add rules like age range or gender.
  • SeasonsOpen a season, set the dates and keep every registration tied to it.
  • Waiver trackingSee which swimmers have a signed waiver at a glance.
  • Document typesAsk for what your club needs. Each upload is checked, then marked verified or sent back.
  • Approvals queueParents and coaches who ask to join wait for your yes.
  • Request to joinAnyone can ask to join from your sign-up page, and nothing happens until you approve.
