Announce it in the app. Email it to the club. Answer questions in a message. Families choose how they hear.

Three ways to reach families.

AnnouncementsPost to everyone or to one group. Families see them on their home screen, and you choose which ones show on your public page.
Email newslettersWrite once, pick the audience, send. aquify works out who gets it from your roster, so there’s no list to paste in and nobody to forget.
MessagesParents write to the club from their phone, and coaches answer in the same thread, with attachments.

Families pick what reaches them.

Parents choose, for each kind of update, whether it comes by email or as a notification. Meet news can arrive instantly while billing notes stay in the portal.

  • Preferences belong to each person, so two parents can choose differently.
  • Everything you post is still in the portal if someone turns the email off.

The family calendar follows them.

Meets, practices, deadlines and club events appear in one calendar. A private link adds it to Apple, Google or Outlook, and it keeps itself current.

  • The link is private to the family and you can turn it off any time.
  • Practices show only for the groups a family’s swimmers are in.
  • Send to a groupAim a message at one group, like Senior One, without anyone filtering a spreadsheet.
  • Delivery you can checkSee how many emails were sent, failed or skipped for each campaign.
  • Reminders for shiftsVolunteers get an email the day before their shift.
  • Your sender nameEmails come from your club’s name and address.
