How we move your club over.

1Send us your exportsDownload spreadsheets of your households, swimmers, groups, best times, open invoices and payments from your current system.
2We import themWe load everything into aquify as a practice run first, so nothing real is at stake.
3We check the numbersCounts of households and swimmers match your export, and the money families owe adds up to the same total.
4You look it overWalk through your own club in aquify, fix what’s off, and decide when you’re ready.
5Families sign inParents get access to their own household. Swimmers can follow once a parent says yes.
