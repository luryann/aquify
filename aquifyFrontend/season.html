A season, start to finish.

Follow one swim season through aquify, from the first sign-up to the last record.

Here’s how a season runs.

1Open registrationFamilies ask to join, you approve, and they register their swimmers.
2Build your groupsSet age ranges, assign coaches, and the roster fills as registrations go active.
3Plan the practicesWrite workouts in shorthand and assign them to each group’s week.
4Take attendanceRoll call on the pool deck. Attendance builds the training picture.
5Collect meet commitmentsFamilies answer yes or no and pick events, with coaches adjusting before the lock.
6Send entries, import resultsThe entry file goes to the host. After the meet, results come back in.
7Bill the clubPreview the run, send the invoices, and take card payments.
8Watch the records fallBest times and records update from every meet.

Then next season starts, and everything you set up is already there.

  • RegistrationSign-ups, waivers, groups and rosters.
  • CoachesWorkouts, roll call and training.
  • MeetsEntries, results and records.
  • BillingDues, discounts and invoices.
