Questions coaches and admins ask.

Straight answers. If something isn’t decided yet, we say so.

The basics

Who is aquify for?

Swim clubs. Coaches use it for workouts and attendance, admins for sign-ups and meets, treasurers for billing, and parents and swimmers for their own pages.

What will it cost, and when can we start?

We haven’t set pricing or a launch date. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you before either is final.

Does it replace TeamUnify or SportsEngine Motion?

That’s what it’s built to do. It covers registration, meets, billing, messaging and training in one place. Some clubs will want to move everything at once, others in stages.

Do we need to be technical?

No. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can run aquify. Each role sees only the screens it needs.

Meets

Which meet files does it work with?

You can build a meet from an SD3, HY3 or ZIP file, import results from any of them, and send your entries back as an SD3 file.

Can parents choose events?

Yes. A parent answers yes or no, and a yes opens the events that swimmer is allowed to swim. Coaches can change entries before they lock.

What if a swim doesn’t match one of our swimmers?

aquify matches by USA Swimming ID first, then name and birthday. Anything it can’t match is listed so you can fix it.

Money

How do parents pay?

By card. They pay on Stripe’s page and the invoice marks itself paid. Families can also turn on autopay.

Does aquify store card numbers?

No. Card details go straight to Stripe.

Can we do sibling discounts, late fees and registration fees?

Yes to all three. Discounts apply the best offer for each swimmer and never stack. Late fees are added once per invoice.

Can I get the numbers into QuickBooks?

Yes. aquify makes a QuickBooks file, and the money reports export as spreadsheets.

Families and phones

Do swimmers need their own accounts?

No. A parent account is all a family needs. Swimmers can have their own login if the club turns it on, and if they’re under 13 a parent has to say yes first.

Can parents see other families?

Never. A parent sees only their own household.

Is there an app in the app store?

No. aquify installs from the browser to a phone’s home screen and opens like an app.

Does roll call work without wifi?

Yes. Marks save on the phone and sync when the connection comes back.

Your data

Where is our information stored?

On servers in the United States.

Can we get our data out?

Yes. The money and roster reports export as spreadsheets, and a family can request a copy of their household’s information.

What happens if a family wants their data deleted?

The parent asks, an admin reviews it, and aquify deletes or de-identifies it. Invoices and payments stay as accounting rules require.

Who can see what?

Admins see everything. Coaches see their groups, practices and meets. Parents see their own household. Swimmers see their own times and schedule.

Switching

How do we move from our current system?

Download your households, swimmers, groups, best times, open invoices and payment history from your current system, and we’ll import them and check the totals with you. The switching page has the details.

What isn’t ready yet?

Pricing and a launch date aren’t set, and we’re still finalizing how moving over works.
