Parents pay in two taps.

Families see what they owe, what’s coming and what they’ve paid, then pay by card. The invoice marks itself paid.

  • Turn on autopay and they see the date and amount before every charge.
  • A statement for each household shows every charge and payment.
  • Card details go straight to Stripe. aquify never stores them.
  • AutopayFamilies choose it, and always see the date and amount of the next charge.
  • Late feesOne per invoice, added for you after the grace period you choose.
  • Credit on accountIf a family overpays, the extra is taken off their next invoice.
  • RefundsRefund a payment, or turn it into credit for the next invoice.
  • Joining mid-seasonCharge for the days left, half the fee, or nothing. You choose.
  • Meet and event feesMeet entry fees land on the family’s invoice, and event tickets get an invoice of their own.
  • ReportsMoney owed by age, revenue, deposits and a QuickBooks file.
